Your library starts here.

A small Go server on your Mac and a native Apple TV app. Your own videos, your YouTube home, and downloads that are guaranteed to play on the living-room TV.

On the shelf

What the Apple TV app gives you today.

▶Local library

A live catalog of your Mac's folders, watched for changes and played straight to the TV with range requests.

⌂YouTube Home & search

Your personalized feed on the big screen, browsed with the Siri Remote.

↓Download queue

yt-dlp jobs that only publish files the Apple TV can actually play. Playlists expand into per-video jobs.

≡Spanish subtitles

Captions picked from available tracks, with source and language kept on every file.

◎Offline first

Downloaded media keeps working with no internet. Nothing leaves your network.

How it works

No cloud, no port forwarding. Everything stays on your LAN.
