Francisco Rivas

Technical Project Manager in Madrid. I turn complex technical work into clear plans, aligned teams and shipped outcomes.

## bio

I'm a husband and a father of two. Family comes first, and my faith is an integral part of my life. It shapes how I work, how I treat people and what I choose to spend my time on.

My work sits between product goals and engineering execution. At Navteca I manage technical projects across AI, cloud and platform engineering, keeping the focus on systems that can actually be operated and maintained once they ship.

Outside work I build things to stay hands-on: a Rubik's Cube trainer for the terminal, a Git identity switcher, a self-hosted media server with its own Apple TV app, and a few AI-powered web apps. They're all on the projects page.

## focus areas

  • Technical delivery · planning, prioritization, dependencies, risk and stakeholder alignment
  • Applied AI · conversational agents, document AI, NLP and AI-assisted workflows
  • Cloud and platforms · AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Helm, CI/CD and observability
  • Engineering operations · issues, pull requests, documentation, release planning
